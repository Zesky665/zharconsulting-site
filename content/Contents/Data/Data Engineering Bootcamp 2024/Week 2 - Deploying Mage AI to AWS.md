## What is EKS?
  
Amazon Elastic Kubernetes Service (EKS) is a managed Kubernetes service provided by AWS. It provides a managed deployment of kubernetes, allowing for easier deployment, management and scaling of resources using Kubernetes without having to manually build out the infrastructure otherwise required.

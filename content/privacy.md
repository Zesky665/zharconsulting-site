Last updated: September 1, 2026

This website (zharconsulting.com) is a static site. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to an absolute minimum.

### What we collect

Nothing. This site:

- does **not** use cookies
- does **not** run analytics or tracking of any kind
- does **not** embed advertising
- does **not** offer accounts, comments, or forms
- does **not** process personal data

### Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub (and its CDN) may process standard technical information such as your IP address as part of serving the page. This is governed by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

### External services

Some pages link to third-party services, for example [Calendly](https://calendly.com/privacy) for scheduling a call. If you use those links or services, their own privacy policies apply — we have no access to data you provide to them.

If you email us, we use your address only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list or share your details with anyone.

### Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
